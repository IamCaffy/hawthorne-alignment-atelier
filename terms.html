About the Atelier

Hawthorne Alignment Atelier (the “Atelier”) is an independent, fan-made character-development tool published by Papalote G Sunshinekisser. It helps players explore a custom 81-point alignment framework, discuss fictional character motivations, and create alignment posters. It is intended for entertainment, creative writing, and tabletop roleplaying.

Interpretive Results

Alignment assessments are subjective interpretations of fictional characters—not official Dungeons & Dragons rules decisions, psychological evaluations, or instructions on how you must play. Results may be incomplete, mistaken, or inconsistent. Players and game masters retain the final say over their characters and campaigns. Posters and other generated materials should be reviewed before public sharing or reuse.
