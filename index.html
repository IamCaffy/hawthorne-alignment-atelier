The 81-Point Alignment Framework

The Atelier evaluates characters on two independent scales, each from 1 to 9. Together, these create 81 possible alignment coordinates and a more nuanced way to describe a character's motivations, principles, and choices.

Lawfulness (X axis)

Measures commitment to a consistent personal code, not simple obedience to laws or authority. From Unrestrained (1) to Absolute (9).

Goodness (Y axis)

Measures concern for the welfare, dignity, and autonomy of others. From Malevolent (1) to Selfless (9).

What the Atelier Does

Players share a character's story, personality, ideals, bonds, flaws, and meaningful decisions. The Atelier offers a reasoned provisional placement, asks clarifying questions when helpful, and lets the player confirm their coordinates before generating a personalized, parchment-style Hawthorne Guild alignment poster.

The goal is to deepen roleplay—not to dictate what a character must do. Alignment can evolve as the character grows.

Getting Started

When the Hawthorne Alignment Atelier is publicly available through ChatGPT's Plugin Directory, players can use it there. Until then, this website provides information about the project; it does not itself generate posters or process character submissions.
