Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Hawthorne Alignment Atelier is a fan-made Dungeons & Dragons character alignment tool distributed as a skill-based plugin for ChatGPT. This policy explains what information is involved when you use it.

Information you provide

You may voluntarily share fictional character descriptions, backstories, personality traits, ideals, bonds, flaws, examples of character decisions, and preferred alignment coordinates. Please avoid including sensitive real-world personal information about yourself or others in character submissions.

How the information is used

The plugin uses the information you provide in ChatGPT to suggest Lawfulness and Goodness coordinates, explain its interpretation, and create a personalized alignment poster if requested.

Processing and storage

The plugin is designed as a skill-based ChatGPT experience with bundled reference material and poster-rendering resources. It does not intentionally send character submissions to a separate developer-operated web service or database. Your conversations, uploaded content, and generated results are processed through ChatGPT, and may be stored or handled by OpenAI according to your account settings and OpenAI's applicable policies.

For details about ChatGPT data practices and available privacy controls, visit OpenAI's Privacy Policy and your ChatGPT settings.

Sharing and visibility

The plugin publisher does not receive a separate copy of your individual ChatGPT conversations through this skill. If you choose to share a character profile or generated poster elsewhere, that sharing is under your control and subject to the destination's privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

The publisher does not maintain a separate database of player submissions for this plugin. To manage or delete ChatGPT conversations and uploaded information, use the controls available in your ChatGPT account. OpenAI's retention and deletion policies apply to content processed by ChatGPT.

Children and fictional content

This plugin is intended for general D&D character discussion and does not specifically target children under 13. Please do not submit personal information about children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the plugin's features or data handling change. The current version will be posted at this address with an updated date.
