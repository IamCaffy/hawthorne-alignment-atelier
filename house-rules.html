Combat

Flanking

A creature gains Advantage on melee attack rolls against a target when an ally is positioned directly opposite it. Both attackers must be within melee reach, and a line connecting their centers must pass through the center of the target's space.

Mounted Combat

Falling from a Prone Mount

When a mount becomes Prone, its rider is dismounted. The rider may use their Reaction to attempt a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, landing on their feet without taking damage from the dismount.

On a failure, or if no Reaction is used, the rider lands Prone and takes normal falling damage based on the distance fallen, if applicable.

Flying Mounts: A successful dismount does not negate any subsequent fall from altitude.
