Caffy's House Rules

D&D 5e — 2024 Ruleset

These house rules supersede the standard rules where applicable.

✦

Flanking: A creature gains Advantage on melee attack rolls when an ally is directly opposite the target. Both attackers must be within melee reach, and a line between their centers must pass through the center of the target’s space.

Silvery Barbs & Black Sap: Neither is permitted.

Silver Horn of Valhalla: Always use the 2024 rules, regardless of the item's listed version.

Healing Potions:

  • Drink (Action): Restore the maximum possible Hit Points.
  • Drink (Bonus Action): Roll healing normally.
  • Administer to Another (Action): Give a potion to a creature within 5 feet; roll healing normally.

Prone Mounts: When a mount becomes Prone, its rider is dismounted. The rider may use their Reaction to make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, landing on their feet without taking damage from the dismount. On a failure, or without using a Reaction, the rider lands Prone and takes normal falling damage if applicable.

Flying Mounts: A successful dismount does not negate any subsequent fall from altitude.
